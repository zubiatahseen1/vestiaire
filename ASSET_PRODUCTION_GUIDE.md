## 📸 Photography Requirements

### 1. Clothing Item Photos (Priority: HIGH)

**Quantity Needed:** 40-50 items minimum for realistic demo

**Categories & Quantities:**
- **Tops:** 10-12 items
  - 3-4 T-shirts (various colors)
  - 2-3 Blouses/Button-ups
  - 2-3 Sweaters/Hoodies
  - 1-2 Tank tops
  
- **Bottoms:** 10-12 items
  - 3-4 Jeans (different washes)
  - 2-3 Dress pants/Trousers
  - 2-3 Skirts
  - 1-2 Shorts
  
- **Outerwear:** 6-8 items
  - 2 Jackets (denim, leather, etc.)
  - 2 Coats (trench, wool, etc.)
  - 1-2 Blazers
  - 1 Windbreaker/Light jacket
  
- **Shoes:** 8-10 items
  - 2-3 Sneakers
  - 2 Boots
  - 2 Dress shoes/Heels
  - 1-2 Sandals
  
- **Accessories:** 8-10 items
  - 2-3 Bags (backpack, tote, purse)
  - 2-3 Scarves
  - 2 Hats
  - 1-2 Belts
  - Jewelry pieces



**Photography Specifications:**
- **Resolution:** 1200x1200px minimum (square format)
- **Background:** Pure white (#FFFFFF) or neutral light gray
- **Lighting:** Soft, diffused (avoid harsh shadows)
- **Format:** PNG with transparency OR high-quality JPG
- **File size:** Keep under 500KB per image (optimize for mobile)
- **Styling:** Flat lay or hanger presentation (consistent across all items)
- **Editing:** Remove background, crop tightly, color correct

**Photography Setup:**
```
Equipment Needed:
- Good camera or smartphone (12MP+)
- Lightbox or white backdrop
- Soft lighting (natural window light or softbox)
- Tripod (for consistency)
- Photo editing software (Photoshop, Lightroom, or Photopea)
```

**Post-Processing Checklist:**
- [ ] Remove background
- [ ] Color correction (accurate representation)
- [ ] Crop to square aspect ratio
- [ ] Center item in frame
- [ ] Slight sharpening
- [ ] Compress for web (maintain quality)

## 🎥 Video Production

### 8. App Demo Video (Priority: HIGH)

**Main Demo Video (90-120 seconds):**

**Storyboard:**
1. **Opening (0-5s):** Splash screen with logo
2. **Problem Setup (5-15s):** Text overlay: "Stressed about what to wear?"
3. **Feature 1 - Wardrobe (15-30s):**

4. **Feature 2 - Weather (30-45s):**
   - Home screen with weather card
   - "Feels like" temperature prominent
   - Outfit suggestion appears
   
5. **Feature 3 - Planning (45-60s):**
   - Creating outfit
   - Adding to calendar
   - Week view preview
   
6. **Feature 4 - Insights (60-75s):**
   - Analytics dashboard scroll
   - Most worn items
   - Cost per wear highlight
   
7. **Closing (75-90s):**
   - Morning routine recap
   - CTA: "Download Vestiaire"
   - App icon + tagline

**Production Specs:**
- **Resolution:** 1080x1920px (vertical/portrait)
- **Frame Rate:** 60fps
- **Format:** MP4 (H.264 codec)
- **Duration:** 90-120 seconds
- **Audio:** Upbeat, calm background music + subtle UI sounds
- **Text:** On-screen captions for key features



**Recording Setup:**
- iOS Simulator screen recording OR
- Real device screen recording (QuickTime via Mac)
- Clean demo data (no lorem ipsum)
- Smooth transitions between screens
- Highlight touch interactions (finger taps visible or animated cursor)

**Editing Tools:**
- Final Cut Pro / Adobe Premiere Pro
- DaVinci Resolve (free option)
- ScreenFlow (Mac screen recording + editing)
- Add device frame mockup in post

## 💰 Budget Estimates

### If Outsourcing:

**Photography:**
- Professional product photographer: $500-1500 for 40-50 items + outfits
- DIY with good equipment: $200-500 (one-time equipment cost)

**Illustrations:**
- Freelance illustrator (Fiverr/Upwork): $300-800 for 12 illustrations
- Icon set: $200-500 for 50-60 custom icons

**Video Production:**
- Professional video editor: $500-1500 for demo + feature videos
- DIY with screen recording software: $0-200 (software cost)

**Total Estimated Budget:** $1,500 - $4,000 for complete asset package

### DIY Approach (Budget-Friendly):

- Use smartphone camera for clothing photos
- Canva Pro for illustrations ($13/month)
- Free stock photos from Unsplash/Pexels
- Screen record with QuickTime (free on Mac)
- Edit videos with iMovie (free) or DaVinci Resolve (free)
- Icons from free sources + customize in Figma

**Total DIY Cost:** $50-300

## Final Tips

1. **Start with the essentials:** App icon, core screens, 40 clothing photos
2. **Be consistent:** Same lighting, same style, same quality throughout
3. **Optimize everything:** Compress images, reduce file sizes
4. **Organize files:** Clear naming conventions, folders by asset type
5. **Version control:** Keep original high-res files separate from optimized exports
6. **Test on device:** View mockups on actual iPhone screens to check legibility
7. **Iterate:** Get feedback early, adjust before producing all assets

**Remember:** Quality over quantity. Better to have 30 excellent photos than 60 mediocre ones!

Good luck with asset production! 📸🎬
