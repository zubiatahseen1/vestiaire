# Vestiaire — Outfit Planning & Smart Wardrobe App

## Overview

Vestiaire is a smart wardrobe and outfit planning app designed to make getting dressed feel easier, calmer, and more intentional.

At its core, Vestiaire is a digital closet where users can organize their clothing, plan outfits ahead of time, and better understand how they actually use the pieces they own. The app combines wardrobe organization, weather awareness, and personal styling into one seamless experience.

What makes Vestiaire especially personal is the reason behind its creation. The app was inspired by the everyday struggle of temperature sensitivity, when weather changes can make someone feel physically uncomfortable, overwhelmed, or even dysfunctional. Vestiaire aims to reduce that stress by helping users dress appropriately for both the actual temperature and the **feels like** temperature outside.

Whether someone runs too cold, overheats easily, or simply experiences decision fatigue while getting ready, Vestiaire is designed to make daily outfit planning more supportive and intuitive.

---

# Features

## Digital Wardrobe Management

Users can upload and organize their clothing items into categories such as:

- Tops
- Bottoms
- Outerwear
- Shoes
- Accessories

Each item can also include:

- Brand
- Color
- Season
- Fabric
- Purchase price
- Notes

This creates a searchable and easy to manage digital version of the user's real wardrobe.

---

## Outfit Planning & Calendar Integration

Vestiaire allows users to create and schedule outfits ahead of time using a built in calendar system.

This helps users:

- Save time in the morning
- Reduce outfit decision fatigue
- Prepare for events or workdays in advance
- Build more intentional styling habits

Users can visually plan their week, organize seasonal outfits, and avoid repeating the same combinations too frequently.

---

## Smart Weather-Based Suggestions

Vestiaire integrates weather data, including the **"feels like" temperature**, to help users dress comfortably and appropriately for changing conditions.

This feature is especially meaningful for people with **temperature sensitivity** — individuals who become extremely uncomfortable when temperatures are too hot or too cold. For many people, temperature changes can affect focus, energy levels, mood, and daily functioning.

Vestiaire helps bridge the gap between weather information and practical outfit decisions by offering smarter recommendations based on real-world comfort rather than temperature numbers alone.

---

## Wardrobe Analytics

Vestiaire tracks clothing usage patterns to help users better understand their wardrobe habits.

Users can view:

- Most worn items
- Least worn items
- Seasonal trends
- Outfit repetition patterns

These insights encourage more mindful wardrobe decisions and help users identify what they truly enjoy wearing.

---

## Cost Per Wear Insights

The app calculates **cost per wear** by dividing an item's purchase price by how many times it has been worn.

This feature helps users:

- Understand the real value of their purchases
- Make smarter shopping decisions
- Invest more intentionally in clothing they genuinely use

---

## Search & Organization

Vestiaire includes advanced search and filtering options that make navigating large wardrobes simple and efficient.

Users can search by:

- Category
- Color
- Season
- Brand
- Usage frequency
- Outfit type

This makes it easy to quickly find clothing items or build outfit combinations.

---

## Shopping Integration

Vestiaire includes external shopping links that allow users to discover replacement items, complementary pieces, or wardrobe additions based on their existing style.

The goal is to create a more connected and personalized shopping experience rather than encouraging unnecessary overconsumption.

---

# User Experience Flow

Vestiaire is designed with simplicity, elegance, and comfort in mind.

Users begin by building their digital closet, then gradually expand their experience by:

1. Organizing clothing items
2. Creating outfits
3. Planning looks on a calendar
4. Reviewing wardrobe insights
5. Receiving weather-aware suggestions

The experience is intended to feel calming, practical, and visually inspiring.

# Why I Built Vestiaire

Vestiaire is deeply personal to me.

One of the biggest reasons I wanted to create this app is because I personally experience temperature sensitivity. Sometimes weather changes can completely affect how comfortable, focused, or functional I feel throughout the day.

I realized that choosing clothing is not always just about fashion — sometimes it is about comfort, regulation, and feeling okay physically.

I wanted to build something that helps people feel more prepared before leaving the house, especially those who struggle with overheating, feeling too cold, or sensory discomfort caused by temperature changes.

Vestiaire is meant to make outfit planning feel supportive instead of stressful.

The app aims to:

- Reduce decision fatigue
- Simplify outfit planning
