## 🎨 Design Principles Reminder

Every design decision should align with these core principles:

1. **Calm & Supportive** — Reduce stress, don't add to it
2. **Weather-Aware** — Temperature integration feels natural
3. **Visually Rich** — Clothing photos are the hero
4. **Insight-Driven** — Data tells a story, not just numbers
5. **Personal** — Feels like it understands individual needs

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Start with Mobile-First:** Design for iPhone first, adapt to larger screens later
2. **Use Real Content:** Avoid lorem ipsum, use realistic clothing names and data
3. **Design in Context:** Show weather cards with real temperatures, realistic dates in calendars
4. **Consistency is Key:** Use components and styles religiously, don't design one-offs
5. **Test on Device:** View designs on actual iPhone screen to check readability
6. **Animation Sparingly:** Only animate what adds value to UX
7. **Get Feedback Early:** Share screens with potential users by Week 2
8. **Document as You Go:** Don't wait until Week 5 to write handoff notes
9. **Version Control:** Use Figma branches for major iterations
10. **Optimize Everything:** Large files slow down development and app performance
